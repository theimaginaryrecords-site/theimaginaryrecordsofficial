About The Imaginary Records

The Imaginary Records is an MSME-registered independent music distribution company from Kashmir, India. TIR distributes independent artists and labels to 200+ platforms worldwide, with mastering, VEVO, Content ID and rights management under one roof. Artists keep up to 85% of royalties, keep their masters, and sign a simple agreement with no lock-in.
