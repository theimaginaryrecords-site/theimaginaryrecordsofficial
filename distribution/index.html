Where your music goes

Every release is delivered to 200+ stores worldwide, including Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, JioSaavn, Amazon Music, Tidal, Deezer and SoundCloud — plus Instagram and Facebook audio for reels and stories, and YouTube Content ID.

What you need to submit

  • Finished audio — WAV or 320kbps MP3.
  • Cover artwork — 3000×3000 px.
  • Metadata — track title, artist name, genre, release date.
  • Splits and credits — writers, lyricists, producers and their shares.
  • Original, unreleased music only — no AI-generated content.
