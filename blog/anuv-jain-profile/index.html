Anuv Jain: India's Indie Storyteller Who Turned Heartbreak Into Anthems

Anuv Jain, born 11 March 1995 in Ludhiana, Punjab, is an Indian singer, songwriter, and composer who has become one of the most popular and streamed Indian indie artists. He is known for simple, acoustic songs built around a guitar or ukulele, with lyrics that deal primarily with love and heartbreak. His best-known works include "Baarishein" (2018), "Alag Aasmaan" (2020), "Gul" (2021), "Husn" (2023), and "Jo Tum Mere Ho" (2024). In 2023, he featured in the Forbes India 30 Under 30 list.

Writing Songs at Sixteen

Anuv began writing songs at sixteen as a way to express his emotions, and lost his father at the age of seventeen. He started uploading original compositions to YouTube in 2012 while still in high school. After moving to Mumbai to study Business Administration, the pull of music proved stronger than any corporate path. He built his career independently, without the backing of a record label, relying on social media and his connection with fans.

The Breakthrough Run

His breakthrough came in 2018 with "Baarishein," a track that announced a new voice in Indian indie music. "Alag Aasmaan" (2020) and "Gul" (2021) followed, each deepening his reputation for intimate, emotionally direct songwriting. Then came "Husn" in 2023, which became one of the most streamed Indian songs of 2024, and "Jo Tum Mere Ho" in 2024. Both "Husn" and "Jo Tum Mere Ho" topped the charts on Apple Music India and Spotify India across all genres.

Signature Sound

Anuv's music is defined by minimalism: an acoustic guitar or ukulele, often unaccompanied by percussion or other instruments, carrying deeply personal lyrics about love, longing, and self-discovery. That simplicity is the point. It has earned him a devoted following among Gen Z and millennial listeners across India and far beyond, and made him a defining figure of the modern Indian indie scene.

Legacy in the Making

Anuv has toured widely, including sold-out shows and his "Guldasta" India tour, and has performed internationally. His live shows are built around the same minimalism as his recordings, with just a guitar or ukulele carrying the songs. His journey proves that an independent artist with honest songs and a direct connection to listeners can reach the very top of the charts without a traditional label machine behind them.
