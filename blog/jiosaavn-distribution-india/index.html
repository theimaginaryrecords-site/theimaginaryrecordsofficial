How to Get Your Music on JioSaavn in India (2026 Guide)

If you're making music in India, JioSaavn is one of the platforms you simply cannot ignore. With well over 100 million monthly listeners — many of them on regional-language and Bollywood playlists — it's where a huge share of Indian music discovery happens. The good news: getting your songs onto JioSaavn is straightforward once you understand the process.

Why JioSaavn matters for Indian artists

Unlike Spotify or Apple Music, where India is one market among many, JioSaavn was built for Indian listening habits. Regional music — Punjabi, Kashmiri, Tamil, Bengali — gets real editorial attention here, and playlists drive a meaningful share of streams. If your audience is in India, JioSaavn streams often rival or exceed what you'll earn on Spotify.

You can't upload to JioSaavn directly

JioSaavn doesn't accept direct artist uploads. Your music reaches the platform through a distributor, who delivers your audio, artwork and metadata to JioSaavn's ingestion system. This is standard for every major streaming platform — you need a distributor in the middle, whether that's a global service or an India-based one like The Imaginary Records.

What you need before you submit

  • Final audio: WAV format, 44.1 kHz / 16-bit minimum. No MP3s — stores reject them or transcode badly.
  • Artwork: 3000×3000 pixels, JPG or PNG, no URLs, social handles or blurry text.
  • Metadata: song title, artist name (spelled exactly as you want it displayed), language, genre, release date, and songwriter/composer credits.
  • ISRC code: the unique identifier for your track. Your distributor assigns this for free — TIR issues ISRCs with every release at no charge.

The timeline: how long does it take?

Once your distributor delivers your release, JioSaavn typically takes 3 to 7 days to ingest and publish it. TIR delivers your music to stores within 24–48 hours of your submission being approved, so most artists see their songs live on JioSaavn within a week. Plan backwards from your release date — submit at least two weeks early so you have time for playlist pitching and artist-profile verification.

Getting your JioSaavn artist profile verified

After your first release goes live, a JioSaavn artist profile is created automatically. You can claim and verify it to get a blue tick, update your profile image and bio, and track your stats. A distributor with a direct relationship with JioSaavn can help speed up verification — this is one of the practical advantages of using an India-based distributor rather than a foreign one with no local presence.

What do you earn from JioSaavn streams?

JioSaavn pays per stream like other platforms, though the exact rate varies by subscription tier and ad load. Indian artists typically see roughly ₹0.08–₹0.20 per stream across domestic platforms. It sounds small, but JioSaavn's volume for regional and Hindi music makes it add up — and with TIR you keep 85% of everything your music earns, paid out to your bank account.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Uploading the wrong artist name spelling — it creates a duplicate profile that's painful to merge.
  • Setting your release date too close — if JioSaavn ingestion takes 7 days and you set a date 5 days out, your release goes live late everywhere.
  • Skipping playlist pitching — JioSaavn's editorial playlists are a major discovery channel; ask your distributor about pitching at least a week before release.
