Free Music Distribution in India: How It Really Works (2026)

"Free music distribution" is one of the most searched phrases by Indian independent artists — and one of the most misunderstood. Let's break down what "free" actually means, where the catches hide, and how to pick an offer that's genuinely fair.

Why would a distributor be free?

Distribution costs a company real money — store deliveries, royalty accounting, support staff. So when a distributor charges you nothing upfront, they're making it back somewhere. The honest models are simple: either they take a percentage of your royalties, or the free tier is limited and the real features cost extra. The dishonest ones hide the cost in the fine print.

The common catches to watch for

High royalty cuts. Some "free" distributors keep 20–30% of everything you earn, forever. On a hit song, that dwarfs any yearly fee you might have paid elsewhere. Always compare the royalty split, not just the signup price.

Paywalled basics. Free upload, but YouTube Content ID costs extra. Free delivery, but it takes 4 weeks unless you pay for "express." Free account, but you can't withdraw royalties below a high threshold. Add up the real cost of the features you actually need.

Lock-in. The nastiest catch: some free distributors make it slow, expensive or nearly impossible to take your music down and leave. Your catalogue becomes hostage. Before uploading anywhere, ask: "If I want to leave in a year, what exactly happens?"

Ownership grabs. Read the terms. A distributor should never ask for ownership of your masters or a share of your publishing. Distribution is a service, not a record deal.

When free distribution makes sense

If you're releasing your first songs and have no budget, a genuinely free distributor with a fair split is a reasonable start. The maths to do: estimate your likely streams, apply the royalty split, and compare against a paid distributor's yearly fee. For most new artists doing modest numbers, no-fee distribution wins.

How The Imaginary Records does it

ISRC/UPC codes, delivery to 200+ platforms, Content ID and royalty splits are all included — not upsells. Our agreement is 6 months per release, stated upfront, and leaving is straightforward. If your music earns nothing, you pay nothing. That's the whole model, and we'd rather you understand it before you sign up than discover it later.

The bottom line

"Free" is never the full story — the real question is what it costs you over time. Compare royalty splits, check the exit terms, and never give up your rights. The cheapest distributor is the one that's honest with you on day one.
