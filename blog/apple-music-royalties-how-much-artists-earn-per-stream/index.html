By Aabid Khan · October 14, 2026

Apple Music Royalties: How Much Artists Earn Per Stream

Apple Music pays more per stream than Spotify — but reaches fewer listeners. That trade-off defines the platform for independent artists. In a 2021 letter to artists, Apple said its average per-play rate was $0.01 and that it pays the same 52% headline rate to all labels. Independent estimates since then generally land in the $0.007 to $0.01 range per stream. As with every platform, treat these figures as estimates: the real rate varies by listener country and plan.

Why Apple Music pays more per stream

The structural reason is simple: Apple Music has no free tier. Every listener is a paying subscriber, so the royalty pool is not diluted by ad-supported listening the way Spotify's is. That is why a smaller number of Apple Music streams can be worth more than a larger number of Spotify streams. The catch is volume — Apple Music has a much smaller user base (roughly 100 million subscribers, versus Spotify's 600 million-plus users), so most artists earn more total dollars from Spotify even at the lower rate.

The 52% headline rate

Apple has said it pays a 52% headline rate to labels, and the same rate to all labels — independent and major alike. That penny-per-play figure goes to rights holders (labels, distributors, publishers), who then split it with artists and songwriters under their own deals. The artist's take-home depends on the contract, not on Apple's rate alone.

Spatial Audio and the Atmos bump

Apple has promoted Dolby Atmos and Spatial Audio mixes, and industry guides report an approximate 10% royalty bump on streams played in Atmos. Delivering an Atmos master is not free, so weigh the cost against your expected Apple Music volume — but if a meaningful share of your audience listens on Apple devices, it can be worth it.

What Apple Music pays on

Apple Music royalties cover both the recording and the composition: the recording royalty flows to your distributor or label, while the performance and mechanical royalties for the song itself flow through your PRO and publishing administrator. Claiming your Apple Music for Artists profile also gives you Shazam data, playlist pitching, and listener insights — free tools that help you understand which songs and cities are driving your Apple income.

What this means for your release strategy

Do not choose between Spotify and Apple Music — distribute to both. Pitch for Apple Music editorial playlists through Apple Music for Artists, claim your artist profile, and make sure your metadata is clean. Apple Music listeners tend to be loyal and high-value; even a modest following there can produce surprisingly solid royalty statements.
