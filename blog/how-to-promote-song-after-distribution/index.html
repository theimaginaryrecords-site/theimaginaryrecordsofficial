Your Song Is Live. Now What? Promotion Playbook for Indian Indie Artists

The Imaginary Records · Promotion · 8 min read

Distribution gets your song onto Spotify and JioSaavn. Promotion gets people to press play. Most independent artists spend 90% of their energy making the song and 10% promoting it — flip that ratio and watch what happens.

Week 1: Ignite your existing audience

  • Your profiles: update bios everywhere with the song link. Pin the announcement post.
  • Stories + reels: post the hook (the catchiest 15 seconds) as a reel on the release day. The hook is everything — lead with it.
  • WhatsApp broadcast: send your song to your personal broadcast list. In India, WhatsApp shares drive real streams.
  • Ask for saves, not just streams: Spotify's algorithm weighs saves, playlist adds, and follows far more than passive streams.

Weeks 2–4: Expand the circle

  • Playlist pitching: submit to independent playlist curators (not just Spotify editorial). Find curators in your genre on Instagram and send a short, polite DM with your link.
  • Creator seeding: send your song to 20–50 small reels/shorts creators in your niche. One viral reel can 10x your streams overnight.
  • College and community pages: meme pages and city pages often promote local artists for free or cheap.
  • Radio: community and online radio stations (including Kashmiri/Kashmir-focused ones for regional artists) still break songs.

Ongoing: Feed the algorithm

  • Release consistently: the algorithm favors active artists. A single every 6–8 weeks beats one album a year.
  • Spotify Canvas + clips: visuals increase shares and saves.
  • Reply to every comment in the first month — engagement compounds.
  • Collab with artists slightly bigger than you; their audience becomes yours.

What NOT to do

  • Don't buy fake streams. Spotify detects botted streams and can remove your song or ban your profile. It's not worth it.
  • Don't spam. Mass-tagging strangers kills goodwill.
  • Don't stop after week one. Most songs grow in weeks 3–8, not week 1.

The 85% rule

Promotion costs money — ads, creator fees, artwork. That's exactly why keeping more of your royalties matters. With TIR's upto-85% artist share, your promo spend earns back faster than on platforms that take 20–30%.
