Too Lost Review: Is It Worth It for Indian Artists? (2026)

The Imaginary Records · Distributor Guides · 6 min read

Too Lost markets itself as the artist-friendly underdog: $19.99/year, 100% royalties paid monthly, publishing admin bundled. Here's what's genuinely good and what the reviews warn about.

What's good

  • $19.99/year Artist plan (or $35.99/year Label with unlimited artists) — strong value.
  • 100% royalties, paid monthly via PayPal, Venmo, Stripe, or ACH — faster than most competitors' quarterly cycles.
  • Publishing admin + copyright registration bundled — services others charge extra for.
  • Royalty advances available.
  • On cancellation, releases stay live (share drops to 85%).

What to watch

  • Support and account freezes: a real pattern in artist reviews — accounts frozen with little explanation, slow responses when it happens. For an artist whose income flows through the platform, this is the scariest possible complaint.
  • Mixed review profile overall: strong value ratings dragged down by support experiences.
  • US-centric payouts: PayPal/Venmo/ACH/Stripe — Indian artists face the usual conversion fees and no INR bank option.
  • Smaller company: less infrastructure than DistroKid/TuneCore if something goes wrong at scale.

Verdict for Indian artists

Too Lost is genuinely good value on paper — monthly 100% payouts with publishing admin at $19.99/year is hard to beat. But the account-freeze complaints are a serious red flag: your distributor holds your money, so support reliability matters more than a $5/year saving. If you try it, withdraw frequently and keep records of everything.
