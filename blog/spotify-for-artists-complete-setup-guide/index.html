Spotify for Artists: Complete Setup and Features Guide

Spotify for Artists is the free dashboard that gives you control over how you appear on Spotify. It is where you manage your profile, read your streaming data, and pitch new music to Spotify's editorial team. If your music is on Spotify and you have not claimed your profile yet, you are leaving your most important storefront unmanaged.

Step 1: Get your music on Spotify first

You cannot claim a profile before your music exists on the platform. Your distributor or label delivers your first release to Spotify, and Spotify creates an artist page for you. Once that page exists, you can claim it. If this is your first release, Spotify may let you request access to the profile once it has received your upcoming release, before it goes live.

Step 2: Claim your profile

Go to Spotify for Artists and choose Claim Your Profile. You will log in with a Spotify account, search for your artist name, and select the correct profile. Spotify then asks you to confirm your identity, usually by connecting your social media accounts or confirming your distributor or label. Spotify reviews the request before granting access, so claim your profile well before release day rather than during release week.

Step 3: Complete your profile

Once approved, fill in everything a new listener sees. Upload a high-quality profile photo and header image, write a bio that describes your sound in plain words, and link your social accounts and website. A bare profile wastes every click that arrives from a playlist. Keep your images consistent with your other platforms so fans recognise you instantly.

Features worth using

  • Analytics: track streams, listeners, saves, listener demographics, and which playlists added your songs.
  • Editorial pitching: submit unreleased tracks for playlist consideration directly from the dashboard.
  • Artist Pick: pin one track, album, or playlist to the top of your profile.
  • Canvas: add a short looping video that plays while your track streams.
  • Concerts and merch: list shows and connect merchandise so fans can act without leaving your page.

Claiming your profile also gives you Spotify's blue verified checkmark, which signals to listeners and curators that the page belongs to the real artist. Set it up once, then update it with every release.

Make a habit of checking your dashboard in the first week after each release. Your audience tab shows which cities, playlists, and age groups are responding, and that information should shape where you promote next. A profile that is claimed, complete, and actively read is the foundation every other Spotify strategy builds on.
