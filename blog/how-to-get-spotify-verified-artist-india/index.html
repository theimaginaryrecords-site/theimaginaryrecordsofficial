How to Get Spotify Verified as an Artist in India (Blue Tick Guide 2026)

The Imaginary Records · Artist Growth · 7 min read

That blue tick next to an artist's name on Spotify isn't just for show. A verified artist profile unlocks Spotify for Artists — your dashboard for pitch playlists, profile customization, audience stats, and pre-save campaigns. Here's exactly how Indian independent artists get verified.

Step 1: Distribute at least one release

Spotify only verifies artists who have music live on the platform. Your distributor (like TIR) delivers your song to Spotify; once it's live, Spotify creates an artist profile for you automatically — usually within 2–5 days of the release going live.

Step 2: Claim your profile on Spotify for Artists

  1. Go to artists.spotify.com and click "Claim your profile."
  2. Search for your artist name and select your profile.
  3. Verify your identity — via your distributor, or with links to your social profiles.
  4. Spotify usually approves within a few days.

What verification unlocks

  • Blue verified checkmark on your profile
  • Playlist pitching: submit unreleased songs to Spotify's editorial team (do this at least 7 days before release)
  • Profile control: bio, photos, artist pick, concert dates
  • Analytics: who listens, where, which playlists drive streams
  • Canvas: add looping visuals to your tracks

Common mistakes Indian artists make

  • Wrong artist profile: if your name matches another artist, your song can land on the wrong profile. Tell your distributor to request a new, separate artist page.
  • Claiming too early: wait until your release is actually live before claiming.
  • Inconsistent names: use the exact same artist name spelling everywhere — Spotify, Instagram, YouTube — so fans find you.

How TIR helps

Every TIR release includes Spotify delivery in 24–48 hours, and if your music lands on the wrong artist profile, our team files the separation request with Spotify for you. Once live, we guide you through claiming Spotify for Artists and pitching your next release to editorial playlists.
