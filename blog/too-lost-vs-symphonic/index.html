Too Lost vs Symphonic: Open Platform vs Curated Partner

By Aabid Khan · October 14, 2026

Too Lost vs Symphonic: Open Platform vs Curated Partner

Too Lost and Symphonic are both popular with independent artists and small labels, but they come from opposite directions. Too Lost is an open platform anyone can join, packed with features at a low price. Symphonic is a curated partner that accepts selected artists and labels and takes a percentage instead of a fee. Here is how they stack up.

Pricing: flat fee vs commission

Too Lost charges $19.99 per year for an Artist account covering one artist with unlimited releases, or $2.99 per month. Its Label account costs $35.99 per year, or $5.99 per month, with unlimited artists — one of the cheapest roster plans available. Symphonic charges no upfront, release, or yearly fee. Instead, it takes an agreed percentage of royalties. The choice is between paying a small fixed fee and keeping everything, or paying nothing upfront and sharing revenue.

Catalogue continuity: what happens if you stop paying?

Too Lost has one of the most artist-friendly continuity policies in the business. Artists who discontinue their subscription can keep their releases in stores and continue receiving 85% of royalties, returning to 100% if they resubscribe. That directly answers the biggest criticism of subscription distribution — losing your catalogue over a missed payment. Symphonic's commission model has no subscription to lapse, so the same problem does not arise.

Features: breadth vs depth

Too Lost bundles royalty splits, YouTube Content ID, publishing administration, catalogue migration, royalty advances of up to 10 years of future earnings, cover licensing, and marketing tools into its plans. It advertises delivery to 450+ stores and platforms, though independent analysis notes that figure counts generously — including duplicate listings and non-store services like Shazam and Gracenote — so treat store counts as marketing numbers, not like-for-like comparisons. Symphonic offers fewer bundled extras but deeper operational tools: monthly payouts, payment splitting with statement breakdowns, video distribution, and label-grade reporting.

Support and reputation

Symphonic is an established company founded in 2006, with a long track record serving labels. Too Lost is newer and founder-led, with a minority private-equity investment, and reviews are genuinely mixed: strong marks for features and price, but a real pattern of support and account-freeze complaints worth reading about before committing a catalogue. Too Lost's payouts require a $50 minimum balance, paid by Wise or PayPal.

The verdict

Choose Too Lost if you want maximum features for minimum money, you run a small label or multi-artist crew, and you value the 85% continuity safety net. Choose Symphonic if you want a curated partner, monthly payouts, and label-grade operations — and you are willing to apply and share a percentage to get it.
