RouteNote vs Amuse vs Ditto: Best Free & Budget Distributor for Indian Artists (2026)

The Imaginary Records · Distributor Guides · 9 min read

Not every artist wants to pay $25+/year upfront. RouteNote, Amuse, and Ditto all court budget-conscious musicians — but their "cheap" plans hide very different trade-offs. Here's the honest breakdown.

RouteNote: free or premium, your choice per release

Free plan: $0, you keep 85% of royalties. Premium: $10/single, $20/EP, $30/album plus $9.99/year renewal — you keep 100%. You can mix: free for experiments, premium for serious singles. Content ID is included. Releases stay live if you stop paying (premium drops to 85% share if you miss renewal). The catch: artists consistently report slow review times and slow support — releases can sit in review for weeks. Also no India caller-tune delivery.

Amuse: the 25% gotcha

Plans at $23.99/$39.99/$59.99/year, all unlimited releases, 100% of streaming royalties. Mobile-first with daily insights and royalty advances. But: 15% Content ID fee (except on Pro), 15% split fee when collaborators aren't subscribers — and the big one: after you cancel, your music stays live but Amuse keeps 25% of all future royalties. That "keep 100%" promise has an expiry date.

Ditto: cheapest subscription, most complaints

$19/year (cheapest of the majors) or $59/year Pro, 100% royalties. But Content ID is Pro-only, Starter releases come down if you lapse, payout minimum is £25 (~$34), and Ditto takes 15% of publishing/sync income. Industry commentator Ari Herstand notes Ditto gets more reader complaints than any other distributor — mostly around support and payouts.

Side-by-side

  • Lowest cost to start: RouteNote Free ($0)
  • Best for high-volume: Amuse ($23.99/yr unlimited) — if you accept the 25% post-cancel cut
  • Cheapest paid plan: Ditto ($19/yr) — with the most support complaints
  • Best catalog safety: RouteNote (stays live) beats Ditto Starter (takedown on lapse)
  • For Indian artists: none offer INR billing or Indian bank payouts as standard; all pay in USD/GBP with conversion costs

The TIR alternative

No yearly fee, upto 85% royalties, INR bank payouts, delivery to JioSaavn/Gaana/Wynk/Hungama plus 200+ global platforms, and a real team in your timezone. For most Indian independents, the math beats every budget option above.
