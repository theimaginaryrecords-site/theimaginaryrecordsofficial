A&R SUBMISSIONS

The Imaginary Records is actively seeking new artists to sign and develop. We accept submissions across all genres.

  • Original, unreleased music only
  • High quality audio (WAV or MP3 320kbps+)
  • Any genre — we value authenticity
  • Include artist name, genre, and a brief bio
  • Response within 24–48 hours
