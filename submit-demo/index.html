How a release moves through TIR

From demo to live on 200+ platforms — six clear steps, no hidden fees, no lock-in.

STEP 02

A&R Review — 24–48h

Every demo is listened to by a real reviewer. We reply honestly, fast.

  • Original, unreleased music only
  • Genuine quality and artistry
  • No AI-generated content
STEP 03

Agreement & Setup

A simple agreement with clear terms — no lock-in, no upfront paid plans.

Delivery

Approved releases are delivered to 200+ stores worldwide.

  • Spotify, Apple Music, JioSaavn
  • YouTube Music, Amazon Music, Tidal, Deezer, SoundCloud
  • ISRC and UPC codes included
STEP 05

Go Live — 24–48h

Your music goes live on 200+ platforms within 24–48 hours of approval.

  • Release-date planning
  • Pre-save and pitch guidance

Royalties

Monthly royalty reports in the artist portal. Transparent, always.

  • You keep up to 85% of royalties
  • Monthly reports and stream data
  • Payouts per the Distribution Agreement

What happens after approval

Approved artists receive a simple agreement (85/15 split, you keep your masters, no lock-in). Once signed, TIR handles delivery to 200+ platforms, ISRC/UPC codes, and monthly royalty reporting in the artist portal.

Review criteria

  • Original, unreleased music only.
  • High-quality audio (WAV or MP3 320kbps+).
  • Any genre — authenticity matters more than trends.
  • Include artist name, genre and a brief bio.
  • Response within 24–48 hours.
