About The Imaginary Records

An independent label and distribution company from Kashmir, built to give local and independent artists a structured path to a global audience.

Founded by an artist

TIR was started by Aabid Riyaz, a musician and producer who releases as Aabyd Khan. He built the label around what he wanted when releasing his own music: speed, clear splits and full ownership.

Frequently asked questions

How long until my music is live?

Most releases go live on 200+ platforms within 24 to 48 hours of approval.

How much of my royalties do I keep?

You keep up to 85% of your royalties and you keep ownership of your masters.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. TIR uses a simple agreement with no lock-in, and you can read the Distribution Agreement before you sign.

Which platforms do you deliver to?

Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, JioSaavn, Amazon Music, Tidal, Deezer, SoundCloud and about 190 more stores.

How do I start?

Use the Distribute button to open the submission portal, or email support@theimaginaryrecords.in with questions first.

Where can I check my royalties?

Log in to the client portal for monthly royalty reports and stream data.
