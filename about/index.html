GLOBAL MUSIC DISTRIBUTION

The Imaginary Records is an independent music distribution company born from the mountains of Kashmir — built by artists, for artists.

We distribute independent artists and labels to 200+ platforms worldwide including Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, and Amazon Music — fast, transparent, and with industry-leading royalty splits up to 95%.

Whether you're a solo artist releasing your first single or a label managing a full catalog, TIR provides the distribution infrastructure, rights management, and support to take your music global — without giving up ownership.
