About The Imaginary Records

An independent music distribution company from Kashmir, India.

What TIR is

The Imaginary Records (TIR) is an MSME-registered independent music distribution company based in Kashmir, India. TIR exists to give independent artists and labels a structured, honest path to a global audience — clear splits, fast delivery, and full ownership of their music.

What TIR does

TIR distributes music to 200+ platforms worldwide, including Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, JioSaavn, Amazon Music, Tidal, Deezer and SoundCloud. Around distribution, TIR offers mastering, VEVO, YouTube Content ID, and publishing and rights management — all under one roof.

How it works

Artists start by submitting a demo through the demo portal. Every demo is reviewed by a real reviewer. Approved releases are delivered to 200+ stores and typically go live within 24 to 48 hours of approval. Royalties are reported monthly in the artist portal.

Distribution partners

TIR distributes through PDL India (Phonographic Digital Limited) and Conyfer — established distribution partners — so releases are handled professionally at every step.

Artist terms

  • Keep up to 85% of your royalties (85/15 split).
  • You keep 100% ownership of your masters.
  • Simple agreement — no lock-in contract.
  • No joining fee and no upfront paid plans; no hidden charges.
  • ISRC and UPC codes included with every release.

Review bar

TIR only works with original artists and genuine quality work. Submissions must be original, unreleased music. TIR does not accept AI-generated content or random submissions.
